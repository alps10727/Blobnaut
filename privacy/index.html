1. Bu politika neyi kapsiyor

Bu politika, Blue Game tarafindan gelistirilen ve Google Play uzerinden dagitilan Blobnaut adli mobil oyunu kapsar. Baska bir uygulamayi, bu sitedeki diger sayfalari veya ucuncu taraf hizmetleri kapsamaz.

2. Toplanan veriler

Hicbiri. Uygulama sizden isim, e-posta adresi, telefon numarasi, konum, kisi listesi, fotograf, cihaz kimligi veya baska herhangi bir kisisel bilgi istemez ve bunlara erisemez. Uygulamanin icinde ag kodu, analiz aracisi veya ucuncu taraf yazilim gelistirme kiti (SDK) bulunmaz.

Uygulamanin Android izin listesi bostur: kurulumda veya calisma aninda hicbir izin talep etmez. Bunu Google Play magaza sayfasindaki “Uygulama izinleri” bolumunden kendiniz dogrulayabilirsiniz.

3. Cihazinizda saklananlar

Oyun yalnizca oyun ilerlemenizi kendi cihazinizda saklar:

  • hangi bolumleri bitirdiginiz ve kac yildiz aldiginiz,
  • topladiginiz altin ve satin aldiginiz karakterler,
  • secili karakteriniz,
  • ses ve titresim ayarlariniz,
  • secili dil.

Bu bilgiler cihazinizin uygulama alanindan disari cikmaz ve bize ulasmaz. Uygulamayi sildiginizde hepsi silinir. Oyun icindeki “ilerlemeyi sifirla” secenegi de ayni kayitlari siler.

4. Android yedekleme

Android, cihaz ayarlarinizda yedekleme aciksa uygulama verilerini sizin kendi Google hesabiniza yedekleyebilir. Bu, Google'in isletim sistemi ozelligidir; yedek sizin hesabinizda durur ve bizim ona erisimimiz yoktur. Cihaz ayarlarinizdan kapatabilirsiniz.

5. Reklam ve analiz

Yoktur. Uygulamada reklam agi, analiz araci, cokme raporlama servisi veya ucuncu taraf takip kodu bulunmaz. Reklam kimliginiz (Advertising ID) okunmaz.

6. Ucuncu taraflar

Uygulama yalnizca cihazinizda zaten kurulu olan Google Play Store uygulamasiyla, yeni bir surum olup olmadigini sormak icin konusur. Bu sorguyu Play Store yurutur, bizim kodumuz degil, ve sonucundan bize hicbir bilgi gelmez. Bu islem Google'in kendi gizlilik politikasina tabidir.

Oyunun dagitimi Google Play uzerinden yapildigi icin, indirme ve guncelleme sirasinda Google'in kendi topladigi veriler Google Gizlilik Politikasi kapsamindadir ve bizim kontrolumuzde degildir.

7. Cocuklar

Uygulama her yas icin uygundur ve hicbir kullanicidan, yasi ne olursa olsun, veri toplamaz. Veri toplanmadigi icin ebeveyn onayi gerektiren bir islem de bulunmaz.

8. Haklariniz

KVKK ve GDPR gibi duzenlemeler, hakkinizda islenen verilere erisme, duzeltme ve silme hakki tanir. Bizim hakkinizda isledigimiz hicbir veri olmadigi icin uygulanacak bir talep de yoktur. Yine de bir sorunuz varsa yazabilirsiniz.

9. Degisiklikler

Ileride reklam veya uygulama ici satin alma eklenirse bu sayfa guncellenir ve degisiklik, ilgili uygulama guncellemesi yayinlanmadan once burada yayinlanir. Sayfanin ustundeki tarih her zaman yururlukteki surumu gosterir.

Privacy Policy

In short: this game collects, stores and shares no data about you. It does not use the internet, it contains no advertising, and it asks for no account. Its Android permission list is empty.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers Blobnaut, a mobile game developed by Blue Game and distributed through Google Play. It does not cover any other application, the other pages of this website, or third-party services.

2. Data we collect

None. The app never asks for and cannot access your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information. It contains no networking code, no analytics library and no third-party SDK.

The app's Android permission list is empty: it requests no permission at install time or at runtime. You can verify this yourself in the “App permissions” section of its Google Play listing.

3. Stored on your device

The game stores only your progress, on your own device:

  • which levels you have finished and how many stars you earned,
  • the gold you have collected and the characters you have unlocked,
  • your selected character,
  • your sound and vibration settings,
  • your chosen language.

None of it leaves your device's app storage or reaches us, and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the app. The in-game “reset progress” option deletes the same records.

4. Android backup

If backup is switched on in your device settings, Android may back the app's data up to your own Google account. That is an operating-system feature; the backup belongs to your account and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device settings.

5. Advertising and analytics

There are none. The app contains no ad network, analytics library, crash reporting service or third-party tracking code, and it does not read your Advertising ID.

6. Third parties

The app talks only to the Google Play Store app already installed on your device, to ask whether a newer version exists. That query is performed by the Play Store, not by our code, and no information from it reaches us. It is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Because the game is distributed through Google Play, whatever Google itself collects during download and update is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and is outside our control.

7. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from any user, of any age. Because no data is collected, there is no processing that would require parental consent.

8. Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK give you the right to access, correct and erase data held about you. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for such a request to act on. You are welcome to write to us with any question all the same.

9. Changes

If advertising or in-app purchases are added in future, this page will be updated and the change published here before the corresponding app update ships. The date at the top of this page always reflects the version in force.
