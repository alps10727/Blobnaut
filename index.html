Oyun

The game

Magara ne yaptigini soyler

The cave tells you what it does

Yesil tutar, mor firlatir, kirmizi oldurur. Hicbir yerde egitim ekrani yok — her yuzey ne yaptigini rengiyle soyler.

Green holds, violet throws, red kills. There is no tutorial anywhere: every surface says what it does with its colour.

Iki dunya

Two worlds

Fosfor Magarasi: yosun, mantar ve hic bitmeyen karanlik. Buz Catlagi: tutunacak yer az, kayacak yer cok.

The Phosphor Cave: moss, mushrooms and a dark that does not end. The Ice Crevasse: little to hold, plenty to slide on.

Uc yildiz, iki yol

Three stars, two ways

Altin hedefi ile atis sayisi birbirini iter: acgozlu oynayan verimli oynayamaz. Uc yildiz tek bir dikkatli tur degildir.

The gold target and the launch count pull against each other. Three stars is never one careful attempt.
